# Domain routing

src/lib/domains.ts owns host classification, route ownership, safe cross-origin links and Auth-origin checks. SurfaceLink maps public navigation, landing CTAs, branding, account-menu links and Customer entry. HostBoundary runs before AuthProvider/private routes mount. Supabase RLS/server authorization remain authoritative; hostname routing is not authorization.

Public paths stay on WWW. Partner/account/business-work paths on WWW move to Partner. Public information links from Partner return to WWW. Customer links open WEB root. Partner Social stays embedded in its Seller/Creator shell. Root Partner resolves to /partner. Customer root offers existing-account login at /login and an accurately unavailable QR entry. Customer /account routes require a real Supabase session created on that origin; they do not require Seller/Creator approval or a fake linked-device flag. Legacy /web resolves to root and /web/login to /login; protected chat/order aliases remain compatible. Explicit Partner links can still leave Customer for business registration.

Cross-origin redirects preserve only seller/creator intent and a restricted path-only next destination. No arbitrary query, Auth code, token, fragment or nested query is forwarded. Customer /auth callbacks are not moved cross-origin; URL session detection is still disabled there (no new email callback allowlist was activated). Signup/reset are centralized on the already-allowlisted Partner origin before requesting email, preserving its PKCE verifier. Exact Partner callbacks stay on their initiating origin. Referrer-Policy: no-referrer prevents callback URLs leaking through navigation or subresources.

Vercel preview and local routes remain path-based. Apex 308 belongs to Vercel; no competing frontend redirect. Catch-all index.html rewrite retains nested-route refresh support. Domain tests cover deep links, callback isolation, preview compatibility and Customer fail-closed behavior.
