# Phase 1 feature scope

> Historical frontend-only scope. The 13 October 2026 connected-platform brief supersedes this list. For current scope, read [WEB_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md](WEB_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [REAL_BACKEND_QA_MATRIX.md](REAL_BACKEND_QA_MATRIX.md). Production routes no longer use fixture authentication, approvals, orders or balances.

## Included

- Partner landing and entry
- Phone/OTP authentication shell with local mock adapter
- Seller and creator role selection
- Seller onboarding, review, submission, and application state
- Creator onboarding, review, submission, and application state
- Conditional professional creator credential step
- Subscription activation interface boundary with no real payment
- Seller Studio operational overview
- Products/catalog, orders, returns, logistics, finance
- Team management and business verification
- Affiliate/creator configuration and analytics
- Account health, storefront, and secure SEO/AI shell
- Creator Center overview, product discovery, promotions, affiliate links, earnings, analytics, and profile
- Repository contracts, mocks, fixtures, responsive behavior, and accessibility foundations

## Excluded

- Consumer Social feed and Stories
- Personal Chat, anonymous chat, and personal AI chat
- Human personal voice/video calls
- Nearby people and consumer discovery
- Games, reward wallet, and mining
- Bill splitting, chit funds, personal expenses, notes, and tasks
- Consumer community, charity, missing-person, and food experiences
- Consumer web messaging/order client (`web.social24-7.com`)
- Live production Supabase access
- Real OTP delivery, document upload, payment, settlement, or AI execution
